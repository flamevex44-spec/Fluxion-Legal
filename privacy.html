Fluxion — Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 9, 2026

Fluxion ("the Bot") is a Discord bot operated by Flux Nodes. This Privacy Policy explains what information Fluxion may collect and how that information is used.

1. Information We Collect

  • Discord User IDs — to identify users and associate them with bot features.
  • Discord Server/Guild IDs — to identify servers where Fluxion is installed and store server-specific settings.
  • Bot configuration data — such as server settings, moderation configuration, ticket configuration, or other settings required for the Bot to function.

Fluxion does not intentionally collect passwords, payment card information, or other sensitive personal information.

2. Moderation Features

Fluxion provides moderation features such as warnings, kicks, bans, and other administrative commands. Depending on configuration, moderation-related information may be associated with Discord User IDs and Server/Guild IDs so that moderation features can function correctly.

3. Ticket Features

Fluxion may process information necessary to operate ticket systems, including the Discord IDs of users and servers involved in a ticket. Ticket messages are not intentionally stored as permanent database records unless a specific feature requires such storage.

4. Music Features

Fluxion provides music-related functionality through Discord voice channels. The Bot may process information necessary to operate these features, such as server, user, voice-channel, and command-related information. Fluxion does not intentionally store users' private voice communications.

5. Message Content

Fluxion may temporarily process Discord messages when necessary to respond to commands or provide its features. We do not intentionally retain ordinary message content as permanent stored data unless a feature specifically requires it.

6. How We Use Information

  • Provide Fluxion's features.
  • Identify users and servers.
  • Maintain server-specific settings.
  • Provide moderation and ticket functionality.
  • Improve reliability and prevent abuse.
  • Troubleshoot technical problems.

7. Data Sharing

Flux Nodes does not sell, rent, or trade collected information. Information may be processed or disclosed when necessary to operate the Bot, protect its infrastructure, or comply with applicable law.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for Fluxion's features and operation. If Fluxion is removed from a server, associated server data may be deleted when it is no longer required.

9. Data Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect stored information. However, no online service can guarantee complete security.

10. Discord

Fluxion operates through Discord and uses Discord's API and services. Your use of Discord is also subject to Discord's policies and Terms of Service.

11. Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. The "Last Updated" date will be changed when significant changes are made.
